This concert meant so much to me, I love Kanye and it meant everything that i got to see him in person. i couldn't think of a better way to enjoy my Halloween. He performed all my favorite songs from can't tell me nothing to all the love. an truly spectacular night that i don't think anything will live up to.   

![IMG_3122.jpg](../../assets/images/IMG_3122-1.jpg)

Watching him perform All Of The Lights to On Sight to All The Love it had me speechless, from the fireworks to the light show, i couldn't believe what i was seeing. I dreamed of seeing Kanye in person for so many years and he finally announced a Houston show and i was able to afford it and live my dream. It kind of sucked going on my own to see him but its okay, it was a great way to enjoy myself.
