I've worked as a Flooring Specialist at The Home Depot in Sugar Land, Texas since May 2024.

- **Technical product consultation:** I walk customers through detailed specs and comparisons so they can make data-informed decisions that fit their needs and budget.
- **Project scoping and cost analysis:** I gather requirements, take measurements, do the calculations and build cost estimates that hold up once work begins.
- **Client-facing support:** I translate technical product information into clear recommendations for people who just want a straight answer.
- **Teamwork:** I coordinate special requests with teammates and supervisors and help keep data and displays accurate.

## Becoming a coach

I became one of the store's coaches, and my photo went up on the Coaches wall. On my card, I wrote that my proudest Home Depot moment was becoming a coach, and that what excites me most is having the responsibility of teaching others.
