In Fall 2024, as part of BUSI 3302: Connecting Bauer to Business, I worked on a consulting-style project with KPMG.

Our team looked at how organizations can better engage and support Generation Z employees, researching ethics, transparency, career progression and mental well-being. We turned that into strategic recommendations and presented them to a panel of judges.
